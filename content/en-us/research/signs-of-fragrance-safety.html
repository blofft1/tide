3 Signs a Company Takes Fragrance Safety Seriously

In a world of confusing claims, how can you tell if a company is truly committed to the safety of its scented products? It’s easier than you think. You don’t have to be a scientist; you just have to know what signals to look for.

Here are three clear signs that a manufacturer takes fragrance safety seriously:

1. They Follow IFRA Standards

Look for a public commitment on their website to following the standards set by the International Fragrance Association (IFRA). This isn't just for show; it's a declaration that they are part of the global safety system. It means they only use fragrance ingredients that have been scientifically evaluated by independent experts and adhere to the strict, product-specific usage rules that IFRA sets.

2. They Offer Ingredient Transparency

While regulations may only require the word "fragrance" on the label, companies that are confident in their safety go a step further. Look for companies that voluntarily disclose information, like through SmartLabel®,where companies can provide a more detailed list of the ingredients used, often down to 0.01% of the formula. This voluntary transparency shows they stand behind their products and respect your right to be informed.

3. They Make It Easy to Contact Them

Check the package for a 1-800 number or a consumer care website. This is more than just customer service; it's a vital part of the safety process called "post-market surveillance." It shows the company is actively listening to consumer feedback and continuously monitoring the safety and performance of their products in the real world.

When you see these three signals, you can feel confident that the company isn't just selling a product – they are demonstrating a deep and ongoing commitment to the science of safety.

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I tell if a company takes fragrance safety seriously?

Look for companies that follow International Fragrance Association (IFRA) Standards, provide ingredient transparency, and make it easy for consumers to contact them with questions or concerns. These practices demonstrate an ongoing commitment to fragrance safety and product stewardship.

What are IFRA Standards?

IFRA Standards are science-based guidelines established by the International Fragrance Association. These standards help ensure fragrance ingredients are used appropriately in different product types and are based on safety assessments conducted by independent scientific experts.

Are laundry fragrance ingredients evaluated for safety?

Yes. Before fragrance ingredients are used in products, they are individually evaluated for safety. Independent scientific organizations review fragrance materials, and industry standards help determine safe use levels for different applications.

What chemicals are in laundry fragrance?

Laundry fragrances are made from blends of individual scent ingredients selected to create specific fragrance experiences. Many companies now provide additional ingredient information through transparency initiatives such as SmartLabel®.

Why does the label only say "fragrance"?

Fragrance formulas are often considered proprietary and may be listed collectively as "fragrance" on a product label. However, ingredient-level safety assessments are typically conducted before those ingredients are used in products.

Are scented laundry products safe?

Companies committed to fragrance safety evaluate fragrance ingredients individually, follow recognized industry standards, and implement safety processes designed to ensure products can be used as intended.

Are fragrance-free detergents safer than scented detergents?

The absence of fragrance does not automatically make a product safer. Product safety depends on the ingredients used, the way they are assessed, and whether they meet established safety requirements for their intended use.

Why is it important for a company to provide ingredient transparency?

Ingredient transparency helps consumers make informed decisions about the products they use. Digital disclosure tools such as SmartLabel® allow companies to voluntarily share more detailed ingredient information beyond what is required on product packaging.

Why should a company provide a customer service phone number or website?

Consumer care channels allow companies to answer questions, gather feedback, and monitor how products perform in real-world use. This ongoing monitoring helps support product quality and safety programs.
