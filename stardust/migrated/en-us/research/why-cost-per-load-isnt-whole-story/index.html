Why Cost Per Load Does Not Tell the Whole Story

What Is the Best Value Laundry Detergent?

The best value laundry detergent is not necessarily the product with the lowest shelf price. A more complete comparison considers whether the detergent cleans effectively in one wash, performs in cold water, and helps avoid added detergent, water, and energy use. The goal is the lowest Cost Per Effective Wash for your household.

Why cost per load is incomplete

Cost per load is useful for comparing the detergent portion of a wash, but it does not capture every expense. Real-world laundry costs can also be affected by wash temperature, the need to re-wash, and choices that may influence garment lifespan.

The Total Cost of Use framework

Total Cost of Use, or TCU, is a framework for evaluating the broader economic picture:

  • Detergent cost: the cost of the recommended dose for the load.

  • Energy cost: the electricity used by the washer, including energy used to heat water.

  • Re-wash cost: the added detergent, water, and energy used when another cycle is needed.

  • Garment lifespan cost: a qualitative consideration for how repeated wash conditions may affect fabrics over time.

TCU is a comparison framework, not a universal price calculator. Household utility rates, washer type, water temperature, dose, load size, soil level, and garment-care needs can all change the result.

The role of Performance Efficacy

Performance Efficacy describes a detergent’s ability to remove a broad range of soils and stains in one wash under different conditions. Strong first-time cleaning can help lower the probability of a re-wash, while effective cold-water performance can help consumers use a lower-energy wash setting when garment-care instructions allow.

From Total Cost of Use to Cost Per Effective Wash

Cost Per Effective Wash, or CPEW, relates Total Cost of Use to the number of successful one-pass loads. It rewards performance that works under the conditions a household actually uses rather than focusing only on the price printed on the package.

Key takeaway

Compare detergent value using the complete wash, not shelf price alone. The most useful endpoint is the Cost Per Effective Wash achieved in your household.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does cost per load not tell the whole story?

Cost per load measures the detergent portion of a wash but does not capture energy use, re-washing, or possible garment-lifespan considerations.

What is Total Cost of Use for laundry detergent?

Total Cost of Use is a comparison framework that considers detergent cost, energy cost, re-wash cost, and garment-lifespan cost.

What is Cost Per Effective Wash?

Cost Per Effective Wash relates Total Cost of Use to the number of successful, one-pass laundry loads.

Is the lowest-priced detergent always the best value?

No. Value also depends on the detergent’s cleaning performance, cold-water capability, recommended dose, and the frequency with which another treatment or wash is needed.
