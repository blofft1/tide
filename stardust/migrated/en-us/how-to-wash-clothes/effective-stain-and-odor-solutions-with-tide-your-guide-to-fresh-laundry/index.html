Effective Stain and Odor Solutions with Tide: Your Guide to Fresh Laundry

Whether it's an old stain, fresh stain, or a mystery smell, identifying the stain or odor type will help you pick the right product to remove it. At Tide, we understand that laundry challenges can be unique, and we're here to offer solutions tailored to your needs.

How to Classify Stains and Smells

Understanding the nature of stains and odors is your first step towards effective removal:

  • Old vs. Fresh Stains: Fresh stains are easier to treat, while older stains may require more effort.

  • Types of Odors: Common odors include urine smells and musty scents. Identifying these helps in choosing the right treatment.

  • Stain Types: From oil stains to food spills, knowing what you're dealing with guides your product choice.

Apply Directly

Use Tide Ultra Oxi or Tide Original Liquid Detergent directly on the stain.

Let It Sit

Allow the detergent to penetrate for several minutes to break down the stain or odor.

Wash Thoroughly

Follow up with a regular wash cycle using Tide's recommended settings for best results.

Picking the Best Laundry Product

Choosing the right Tide product can make all the difference (US Magazine):

  • For Oil Stains: Tide Ultra Oxi is formulated to tackle tough grease and oil stains effectively.

  • For Urine Smells: Tide Oxi Boost Power Pods offer a powerful clean that helps neutralize odors.

  • General Use: Both Tide Ultra Oxi and Tide Oxi Boost Power Pods deliver trusted results for everyday laundry needs.
