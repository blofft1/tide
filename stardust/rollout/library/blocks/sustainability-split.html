How does Tide do this?

Simply put, a concentrated formula gives you more active ingredients per dose in a smaller package without sacrificing cleaning power. This means that the cleaning power of Tide is stronger in each compact detergent dose than in previous formulas, which required larger doses. To achieve this, we re-formulate the cleaning ingredients to ensure they perform more efficiently than they did before.
