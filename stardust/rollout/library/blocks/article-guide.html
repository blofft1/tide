Apply Directly

Use Tide Ultra Oxi or Tide Original Liquid Detergent directly on the stain.

Let It Sit

Allow the detergent to penetrate for several minutes to break down the stain or odor.

Wash Thoroughly

Follow up with a regular wash cycle using Tide's recommended settings for best results.
